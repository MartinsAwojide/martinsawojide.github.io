AI Engineer and Lean Six Sigma Black Belt — building agentic workflows, deploying models on edge hardware, and applying process engineering to make intelligent systems work in the real world. Background in manufacturing and industrial data science; MS from Carnegie Mellon.

Before graduate school I spent several years in Nigerian manufacturing — warehouse operations, Six Sigma projects, and building the data infrastructure to support both. That grounding shapes how I approach AI: less about the model, more about the system it lives in. Interested in agentic AI, edge deployment, and where the two meet.

My first degree is in Chemical Engineering — process systems thinking, feedback control, and reaction kinetics are the original languages of "how do complex systems behave and how do you steer them." That framing transfers naturally to AI for science: agentic workflows for material discovery, automated laboratories, and machine learning for physical processes.
